Price Bird
GitHub

Price alerts in Telegram, written as one line.

Price Bird watches tokens, CEX pairs, market caps and NFT floors, and messages you when your rule triggers. Open source. Runs on your own server.

Open in Telegram View source
you 
/alert BTC 10%
3 h later 
🔔 BTC ↑ +10.3%

Rule: 10% move up or down
Price: $69,642
Market cap: $1.37T

Source: Binance
Links: TradingView

Self-host

One image, three services. migrate applies the schema, bot talks to Telegram, worker fetches prices and sends messages. Bring your own Postgres and Redis, or start the bundled ones with the infra profile. 

git clone https://github.com/Sergio-prog/price-bird
cd price-bird
cp .env.example .env
docker compose --profile infra up -d --build

Fill BOT_TOKEN in .env first. Registration stays allowlist-only until you set PUBLIC_ACCESS_ENABLED=true.
